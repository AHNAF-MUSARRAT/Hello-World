Explore The Magical Beauty Of Pabna, Bangladesh

Bisnakandi

Bisnakandi is a breathtaking landscape where the lush, green mountains of Meghalaya meet a rushing mountain river. Located in Gowainghat, it serves as a natural stone quarry where crystal-clear water cascades over a vast bed of smooth, white rocks. Visitors can wade through the cool, shallow currents while taking in the stunning sight of clouds hanging over the surrounding Indian hills.

Lalakhal

Lalakhal is famous for its mesmerizing, turquoise-blue water that changes shades depending on the season. Situated in Jaintiapur, this wide river flows down from the Jaintia Hills. Taking a boat ride along Lalakhal offers a tranquil escape, flanked by thick green forests, small local villages, and hills on both sides, making it a favorite for photography lovers.

Madhabpur Lake

Madhabpur Lake is a serene, man-made reservoir nestled deep within the rolling hills of the Madhabpur Tea Estate in Moulvibazar. Surrounded by endless rows of vibrant green tea bushes, the lake's calm water is often blanketed with rare blue water lilies. It is an ideal spot for peaceful walks, birdwatching, and enjoying panoramic views of the valley from the hilltop trails.

Jaflong

Jaflong is one of Sylhet’s most famous border attractions, located at the foot of the Khasi hills. It is renowned for its dramatic landscape, where the Piyain River carries millions of boulders down from the mountains. Visitors flock here to see the majestic Dawki Suspension Bridge stretching across the border, watch local stone collectors at work, and explore the nearby indigenous Khasi tribal villages.
