CAR TECHNOLOGY

Modern cars contain many technologies designed to improve performance, comfort and safety.

ABS

Anti-lock Braking System helps prevent the wheels from locking during hard braking.

Airbags

Airbags are an important safety feature designed to help protect occupants during crashes.

Aerodynamics

Aerodynamics studies how air moves around a car and can improve efficiency and stability.

Parking Sensors

Sensors can help drivers detect objects around a vehicle while parking.

GPS Navigation

Navigation systems use location information to help drivers find routes and destinations.

Driver Assistance

Cameras and sensors can support features such as lane warnings and emergency braking.
