ELECTRIC CARS

Electric vehicles use electric motors instead of traditional petrol or diesel engines.

Electric Motor

Electric motors convert electrical energy into movement and can provide quick acceleration.

Battery

Batteries store electrical energy used by the vehicle's motor.

Charging

Electric cars can be charged at home or at public charging stations.

Regenerative Braking

Some electric vehicles can recover energy during braking and send it back to the battery.

THE FUTURE OF CARS

Electric vehicles are becoming an important part of the automotive industry. Improvements in batteries, charging and software are helping electric vehicles develop further.
