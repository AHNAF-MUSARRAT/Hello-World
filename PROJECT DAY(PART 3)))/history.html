CAR HISTORY

Cars have developed enormously since the first practical automobiles appeared.

1880s — EARLY AUTOMOBILES

During the late 19th century, inventors developed practical motor vehicles powered by internal combustion engines.


1900s — MASS PRODUCTION

Mass production made automobiles more accessible and helped transform transportation.


1950s–1980s — PERFORMANCE

Sports cars and motorsport became increasingly popular, leading to many famous performance cars.


2000s–TODAY — SMARTER CARS

Cars now use advanced computers, sensors, electric powertrains and driver-assistance systems.
