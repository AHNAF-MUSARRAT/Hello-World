CAR RACING

Racing is one of the most exciting parts of the automotive world.

Formula 1

Formula 1 is a high-level single-seater racing championship. Teams compete using highly engineered racing cars.

Rally

Rally cars compete on different surfaces such as gravel, snow and tarmac.

Endurance Racing

Endurance races test both the speed and reliability of cars over long distances.

WHY IS RACING IMPORTANT?

Racing allows manufacturers and engineers to test new ideas. Technologies developed for motorsport can sometimes influence road cars.
